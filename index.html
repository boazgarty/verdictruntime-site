How it works

Find it. Prove it. Fix it. Protect it.

One loop from first scan to final verdict. Every step is optional: use only what you need.

  1. 01 · SCAN

    See the whole surface

    Code, dependencies, secrets, infrastructure, licenses and containers in one pass, or bring your existing SAST results as SARIF.

  2. 02 · PROVE

    Verify against the live app

    A reviewed, non-destructive proof runs in an isolated sandbox. No reproduction, no “confirmed”.

  3. 03 · PENTEST

    Test it like an attacker

    Logged in as a real user: API discovery and fuzzing, access-control tests, and LLM security checks.

  4. 04 · FIX

    Ship a verified fix

    A reviewable diff or pull request, re-scanned and tested. Never merged on your behalf.

  5. 05 · DEFEND

    Shield it meanwhile

    WAF, gateway and detection rules generated from your findings, in observe mode first.

FAQ

Questions security teams ask first

Does my source code leave my network?

Not if you self-host: the scanner runs in your environment and only results leave, if you choose to send them. With the hosted option, your code is deleted automatically within an hour of the scan, or immediately on request.

What does “confirmed” actually mean?

A hand-written, reviewed proof reproduced the issue against your running application, inside a sandbox whose network access is locked to your target. If it doesn't reproduce, it isn't called confirmed. It stays labelled with the evidence it does have.

Do you test production?

We test only what your written scope names, and we recommend a staging environment. Live traffic, logged-in testing and anything that writes data each need their own explicit authorisation.

Do I have to use every step?

No. Scan, verify, pentest, fix and defend are independent. Start with a scan, or bring your existing scanner's results and use us only for proof and fixes.

Will you deploy defense rules or merge fixes for us?

No. Fixes arrive as a reviewable diff or pull request, and defense artifacts are generated for your team to review and deploy, starting in observe mode.

How is pricing set?

Per engagement, quoted up front: by the number of repositories and applications in scope, whether live and logged-in testing is needed, and whether you have AI features to test. The first assessment is free.
