How it works

Find it. Prove it. Fix it. Protect it.

One loop from first scan to final verdict. Every step is optional: use only what you need.

  1. 01 · SCAN

    See the whole surface

    Code, dependencies, secrets, infrastructure, licenses and containers in one pass, or bring your existing SAST results as SARIF.

  2. 02 · PROVE

    Verify against the live app

    A reviewed, non-destructive proof runs in an isolated sandbox. No reproduction, no “confirmed”.

  3. 03 · PENTEST

    Test it like an attacker

    Logged in as a real user: API discovery and fuzzing, access-control tests, and LLM security checks.

  4. 04 · FIX

    Ship a verified fix

    A reviewable diff or pull request, re-scanned and tested. Never merged on your behalf.

  5. 05 · DEFEND

    Shield it meanwhile

    WAF, gateway and detection rules generated from your findings, in observe mode first.
